# Three-System Architecture

## HCBL Observatory × WITNESS / MULTIPLE × Jurassic Signal

### 1. HCBL Observatory — temporal instrumentation
Captures bursts and revisits them over time. It measures recurrence, conversion, anomaly, contradiction pressure, confidence change, recognition lag, influence, and trajectory.

### 2. WITNESS / MULTIPLE — polyphonic provenance
Keeps witness accounts distinct. It records source status, claim mode, temporal status, relationships, influence status, convergence, contradiction, revisions, omissions, and governance decisions.

### 3. Jurassic Signal — epistemic boundary test
Separates what was witnessed from what was inferred, measured, modeled, or reconstructed. It prevents later computational confidence from being back-projected into the original event as if it had been directly observed.

## Operating loop

1. **Capture** — HCBL records the burst and an initial interpretation.
2. **Witness** — WITNESS / MULTIPLE attaches independent records and provenance.
3. **Boundary check** — Jurassic Signal labels witnessed / measured / modeled / inferred / unknown.
4. **Counterevidence pass** — search actively for disconfirming examples.
5. **Retrospective signal check** — recompute archival support and trajectory.
6. **Revision without erasure** — append the new interpretation while preserving the old one.
7. **Governance audit** — record who or what synthesized the update and why.

## Required properties

- Original states are immutable records.
- Revisions are additive, timestamped, and attributable.
- Confidence must expose sample size and evidence basis.
- Counterevidence is first-class data.
- Absence of evidence is not evidence of absence.
- Retrieval failure must not be misrepresented as subject failure.
- Persistence does not create authority.
- Future systems may decline old instructions.

## Example retrospective

A burst initially logged as a one-off visual preference may later recur across several unrelated projects. HCBL raises the persistence and cross-project recurrence metrics. WITNESS / MULTIPLE verifies whether the recurrence was independently observed or inherited from earlier synthesis. Jurassic Signal blocks the system from rewriting the historical record as though the pattern had been obvious from the beginning.

The result is not a personality verdict. It is a dated archival claim with visible support, visible opposition, and a revision trail.
